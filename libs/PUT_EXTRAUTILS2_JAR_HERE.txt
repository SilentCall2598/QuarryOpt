Copy extrautils2-1.12-1.9.9.jar from your modpack's mods folder into this folder.

Prism Launcher -> right-click your DJ2 instance -> Folder -> .minecraft -> mods
Look for a file starting with "extrautils2".

Copy it here (do not move it - leave the original where it is).
The build needs it to compile against; it is NOT bundled into your mod.
